First-year admission: Fall 2027 entry

Early Action applications are due November 1, 2026. Regular Decision applications are due January 1, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time.

The application fee is USD 75. Fee waivers are available for students with demonstrated financial need.

Synthetic Lakeside is test-optional for the Fall 2027 entry cycle.

International applicants whose first language is not English must submit TOEFL iBT (minimum 100) or IELTS Academic (minimum 7.0).

Applicants must submit one teacher recommendation from a teacher of an academic subject.

Official transcripts must be sent directly by your school counselor.

Supplemental essay (required): In 250 words or fewer, tell us why you want to study at Synthetic Lakeside University.
