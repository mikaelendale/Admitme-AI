## What "Unlimited" Actually Means (With Fair Use)


| Feature      | Sprint & Pro Limit           | Why                                                 |
| ------------ | ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Gap analysis | 30 per 30 days               | Covers 99% of users. 1 user doing 100 gaps = abuse. |
| Essay roast  | 30 per 30 days               | Most students write 5–10 essays total.              |
| AI chat      | 500 per 30 days              | ~17 messages/day. Normal.                           |
| College list | 10 regenerations per 30 days | List doesn't change daily.                          |
| Export       | 5 per 30 days                | Enough for college list exports.                    |

## What Resets When


| Plan   | Reset Rule                            |

## What Happens at Limit


| Threshold        | User Experience                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **80% used**     | NoCap warns: "You've used 24 of 30 gap analyses this month. Reset on May 1."                                      |
| **100% reached** | NoCap says: "You've hit your monthly limit. Upgrade to Pro Annual for higher limits, or wait until [reset date]." |
